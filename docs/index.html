Every Feature of Practice Mod 1.0

A full set of fixes, practice tools and improvements made for the most demanding speedrunners.

🎥

Instant Camera on Load Pos

The camera interpolation is cancelled right away (al::requestCancelCameraInterpole) on Mario and the scene, for 10 frames.

  • Locks onto Mario instantly on the first frame
  • Leftover speed reset to zero (no unwanted momentum)
  • Full support of captures (Frog, Goomba, etc.)
🌈

Free & Multicolor Input Display

A fully customizable controller display on screen.

  • Drag & Drop with the mouse or touch right on the screen
  • 4 corner presets and fine tuning in steps of 25 pixels
  • Multicolor (RGB Rainbow) color with a smooth animated wave
  • Full 2 Player mode support (independent P1 & P2)
🔄

Reload at Pos / Instant Reload

Reloads the whole scene while putting Mario back at his exact current coordinates and rotation.

  • "Reload at Pos" option (Yes/No) in Misc and Options
  • "Restart at Pos" action button
  • Robust state machine against desyncs (mReloadState)
  • Health restored and cutscenes skipped automatically
🪙

The Game's Real 8-bit Luigi

In the official game, some bushes and grass tufts have a 10% chance to hide an 8-bit Luigi. The "Luigi Pixels" option forces that roll to 100%.

  • 100% official animation, coins and sounds: the game itself makes Luigi appear
  • 28 official spots across 8 kingdoms
  • Reload the scene after turning the option on testing
🐰

Skip Broodals

As soon as the intro cutscene ends, the boss is beaten through its own "last hit" code: the game plays the ending cutscene and spawns the Multi Moon as usual.

  • Madame Broode (Cascade Kingdom), Hariet (Sand Kingdom), Rango (Lake Kingdom)
  • Spewart (Wooded Kingdom, and the rematch in the Luncheon Kingdom)
  • Also works on the rematches without a Multi Moon testing
🔒

Inputs Blocked in the Menu

While the vertical menu is open, every controller button and stick is cut off from the game.

  • Mario stays perfectly still while you browse
  • No accidental jump, camera move or Cappy throw
  • Keybinds cannot fire inside the menu
🎩

Outfit Picker (42 Outfits)

Change your look instantly without going to the Crazy Cap store.

  • "Outfit Body" picker (all 42 SMO outfits)
  • "Outfit Cap" picker (42 different hats)
  • "Apply Outfit" button that reloads Mario in his new outfit
⚡

Built-in Speedrun Refreshes

Every practice switch to reset the world:

  • Cutscene Refresh: brings back the cutscenes and NPC talks (Captain Toad, Hint Toad, Jammin' Toad, Traveling Peach)
  • Doors, Moon Shards, Purple Coins & Gray Moons: full respawn
🎮

Translucent Rodin 1.3 Vertical Menu

The modern and clean design of Practice Mod 1.3, adapted to the native 1.0 features.

  • 8 organized categories: Options, Stages, Misc, Timer, Input Display, Keybinds, Info, Settings
  • Options category on 2 columns to see everything at once
  • Official white high-resolution Rodin font
  • Quick D-Pad shortcuts (Save, Load, Kill Scene)

Mod folder location

Turn it on in Yuzu

In Yuzu, right-click Super Mario Odyssey → Properties → check Practice mod 1.0 (or Practice mod BTT 1.3 if your game is on 1.3.0). Only check one of them.

Controller Shortcuts & Quick Controls

Take full control of your practice sessions with simple, intuitive combinations.

Open / Close the menu L + D-Pad Left
Save position (Save State) D-Pad Left
Load position (Load State) D-Pad Right
Reload the scene (Kill Scene) D-Pad Up OR ZL + ZR
Previous scene (Prev Stage) D-Pad Down
Quick action wheel Hold R3 (Right Stick)
